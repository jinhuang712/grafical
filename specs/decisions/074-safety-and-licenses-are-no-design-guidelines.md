# 074. Safety and licenses are no design guidelines

Decided: 2026-11-06

## The problem

Two guidelines came from no stance: a diagram reads no file outside its folder and its pictures run nothing, and every dependency allows use in an MIT project. The owner holds that both are basic.

## The choice

Both leave the design guidelines. The features and specs keep what a mark may read, what an SVG may hold and where the viewer listens. The list of allowed licenses becomes a rule of how the work is done.

## Not chosen

- A stance for them to come from.
  - They are no belief about diagrams.
- Keeping them as guidelines with no stance.
  - Each guideline should trace back to a stance.

## Consequences

A new design is no longer asked these two questions by a guideline, so a review has to remember them.
