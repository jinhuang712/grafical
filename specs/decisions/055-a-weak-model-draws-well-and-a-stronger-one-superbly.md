# 055. A weak model draws well and a stronger one superbly

Decided: 2026-11-05

## The problem

The philosophy promised pictures that are easy and consistent, while the MVP bar asked Haiku to redraw a picture and beat the original. Copying a picture well tests fine tuning, and fine tuning takes thought. The owner said that a weak model also draws well, and that drawing very well still needs a stronger model.

## The choice

- **Haiku holds the floor.** A weak model's picture is already good.
- **A strong model reaches for the top.** Opus redraws the reference pictures.
- **grafical supplies no taste.** It owes that model the means to tune what it wants.

## Not chosen

- Haiku copies the pictures.
  - grafical would have to supply the taste that fine tuning takes.
- Copying leaves the MVP bar and is only recorded.
  - Drawing very well is one of the project's aims.
- A weak model's picture that is only good enough to use.
  - The owner holds that it draws well.

## Consequences

A failed copy by Opus points to what the tool cannot say, and not to a model's limit. The means of fine tuning become part of the aim, so how freely an agent can adjust a picture stops being a side question. Copying costs Opus runs.
