# Changelog

What changed in each release of grafical, from the side of the agent that draws and the person who asks.

## Unreleased

grafical has not been released; the first release is 0.1.0 (ROADMAP.md, "Release 0.1.0").

- `group title <file> <group> <place>` (or `group label`) says where a group's label sits: `top-left`, `top` or `top-right` in its head, `bottom-left`, `bottom` or `bottom-right` in a foot below its last row, `left` or `right` beside its rows inside the box, or `outside-top`, `outside-bottom`, `outside-left` or `outside-right`, centred just outside the box; the file holds it as the group's `titleAt`, a batch as `group.title`, and `show` prints `title=<place>`. The label sits exactly there and takes only its own room; no box or line moves for it. A line that would run across it refuses the write as `title-blocked`, which names the lines in the way and, as `clear`, the places none comes near. Without a place the label is seated as before.
- `edge step <file> <id> --n <number>` numbers a line as a step of a process, from 1 to 99, and grafical draws the number in a small ring on the line, under the start of its label. Without `--n` the number is cleared; in a batch it is `edge.step`. `show` prints `[step <n>]` after the line, and `show --drawn` adds `step=<n>` to its record. A numbered line keeps its own port, and its label's text stands a little further from the line, so a picture with numbers grows a little taller. `check --all` reports a badge that touches anything as `step-collision`.
- A line that runs straight between two boxes centred one under the other now meets them at their centres more often: where other ports or the lines sharing its way had pushed it a little aside and nothing else would move or bend, it is put back.
- A row with no boxes may be a line's end, such as a gateway or a message bus: it is drawn as one 40 px bar across the picture with its name in it, and lines meet it straight past their other ends. It was refused as `empty-container`. `layer look <row> box|band` draws a bar or a band as a box or as a band; a bar is a box unless it says band. A line that passes a bar without ending at it is refused as `bar-crossed` unless `edge add --pass` or `edge pass` lets it; `bar-to-bar` and `bar-in-group` refuse a line between two bars and a bar inside a group's rows.
- `group order` moves the boxes of a group's inner groups with its own, as one block: two zones whose boxes all sit in subnets now swap, where they were refused as `no-shared-layer`. A group ordered beside itself, or beside a group around or inside it, is refused as `bad-reference`.
- A line between two boxes of one row may run above the row instead of under it, when fewer lines meet the boxes between its ends from above than from below: it then crosses fewer lines. It stays below where its box lies under a group's label, where a wall or a lane's border lies above the row, and where going above would bend another line or push a label out of its place.
- `group copy` copies a group with its inner groups, its boxes and the lines between them, every id with `--suffix`, before or after a group in each of its rows: a second zone, region or shard in one operation. `--outside` copies its lines to everything else too.
- `show --drawn` prints a `gutter` record for each pair of neighbouring rows, with the lines, crossings, tight pairs and crowded labels between them.
- The `counts` record gains `across=` (lines through a group holding neither of their ends), `tight=` (pairs of runs of two lines side by side under 12 px apart for over 100 px) and `crowded=` (line labels with under 8 px of room on two or more sides). `apply --help` describes them.
- `grafical try <file> <batch.yaml>...` takes several batches: one `before` record, then each batch's `ok`, `after` and `delta` records keyed by `batch=<file>`.
- An unknown option, a missing argument or an unknown command prints `error code=unknown-option found=--bogus` (exit 2) instead of the parser's sentence.
- `node add` into a row that already has boxes, and `node move --layer`, need a place: `--first`, `--last`, `--before` or `--after`. Without one the write is refused as `missing-field`, with the row and its boxes. grafical no longer picks the slot.
- `layer tidy` and `diagram tidy` are removed. Compare orders with `grafical try` instead.
- The legend says only what the agent named: line styles and widths lose their default words ("call or read", "message", "optional", "related", "main path"), and a diagram that names nothing draws no legend. `show --drawn` prints a `legend` record with `text=-` for each style and width left unnamed, with the `weight` to name it by. `diagram legend --hide` is removed; an old file's `false` is read as no words.
- A group's label makes room between the lines into its first row instead of pushing a line off its box's centre: the first two boxes with such lines move apart until the label fits between them, so a picture may grow a little wider.
- Several lines may turn into one side of a box, each at its own height, the nearer from above entering higher: a second line into a side no longer bends twice into the box's top.
- The help teaches composing an architecture diagram: each system a group, the groups side by side with each entry above its stores; boxes that talk next to each other; a row that lines end at, drawn as one band, for what every request passes, such as a gateway; a queue as a box only when it is the story, otherwise its events as lines between the services; and side paths cut. To stay within the help's length, `grafical --help` no longer repeats what a refusal looks like: `grafical apply --help` says it.
- A line between two neighbours in a row runs straight across the gap with its label above it for labels up to 200 px wide, about thirty characters; the limit was 120 px, and a longer label sent the line round under the row.
- A line that ends at a row meets its band straight past its other end where nothing is in the way, instead of turning toward a box inside the band first.
- A label that must leave its best place no longer pushes another line across a fan-out: the place it takes is weighed by the crossings it truly forces.
- Two lines that leave one side of a box and turn into boxes on either side of it now both turn once: the second leaves a little off the side's middle, where it bent twice before.
- Two lines may now turn through the same gap between two boxes of a row, by their sides, when their runs along the row keep apart. A line that bent twice because the gap was taken now turns once.
- A line between neighbouring rows runs straight when the lower box's other ports, or a group label above it, push its lower end off the upper one: the upper end moves to meet it, where nothing else in the gutter runs across.
- A line's label no longer lies across the border of a group box, and `check --all` reports one that does as `text-crossing`, naming the group and the line.
- A line's label no longer lies across the border of a lane or a band either, and `check --all` reports one that does as `text-crossing`, naming the lane or the row and the line. A labelled line that crosses from one column lane into the next moves their shared side close to the first lane, and its label sits past the side, so a picture with column lanes may grow a little wider.
- Columns hold across rows: every box of a column now sits left of every box of the next column in any row, not only in its own row. A picture that uses columns may grow wider.
