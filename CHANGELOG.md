# Changelog

What changed in each release of grafical, from the side of the agent that draws and the person who asks.

## Unreleased

grafical has not been released; the first release is 0.1.0 (ROADMAP.md, "Release 0.1.0").

- Every refusal, defect and warning prints as a block after the outcome line, a blank line before each: its kind (`error`, `defect` or `warning`) and code, `description:`, one fixed sentence per code that says what happened, `source:`, what caused it (a warning has none), `detail:`, its facts one per line, and `next:`, the names of the commands that can change it, or `-`. A usage error prints `failed changed=-` first too. `--json` gives each as `{kind, code, description, source, detail, next}` under `problems`. The one-line `error op=... code=...` and `defect code=...` records are gone, and so is every `fix`: no refusal writes the command for the agent any more. `elements=` gives way to typed keys (`node`, `edge`, `group`, `row`, `lane`, `wall`), and `likely` is a value only: `grafical help apply` gives `found: help` and `likely: --help`.
- A refusal's `source` is the command line as typed; for an operation of a batch, `<batch file> line <n>: <the operation as written>`; for a problem the file had before the command, `already in the file`. A defect found after layout, or an error found only in the diagram the whole batch makes, names the batch line after which it first appears and stays, where it named the batch's last operation; where no line can be known, because the prefixes before it are refused for other problems or the prefixes a refused write may try are spent, it names the batch file alone.
- Every write, single commands included, and `check` and `render` print every warning the diagram has, each a block: `bent-line` (a line with more than two bends), `passing-row` (a line through rows it does not connect), `line-across-group` (a line through a group holding neither end, with the groups), `tight-lines` (two lines under 12 px apart for over 100 px), `crowded-label` (a label with under 8 px of room on two sides), `lonely-box` (a box no line reaches, neither its own nor one to its group, row or lane) and `crossing` (a pair of lines that cross). A warning has no source: the outcome line names what the write changed. The counts are as before. On a 50-box draft a write prints about 170 to 230 such blocks, most of them crossings.
- Refusals carry what an agent needs to find the change itself: `bar-in-group` names the bar's row, the group, the line and the group's `top` and `bottom` rows, and `group-conflict` those rows too; `alignment-conflict` names the boxes in the way; `edge-inside`, `edge-blocked`, `bar-to-bar` and `bar-crossed` name the line's `ends`; `group-across-lanes` names a box in another lane and that lane.
- The help pages no longer have a "When refused" section, `apply --help`'s included: a refusal's block says what happened, what caused it and which commands can change it. `apply --help`'s "Output" describes the block, the outcome of a refusal and the exit codes.
- The help no longer puts a "Not shown: ..." footnote in front of every agent: step 1 of `grafical --help`, the examples of `new --help` and `apply --help`, and `new --note`'s description no longer show one. In the 2026-10-18 round, 18 of 22 drafts wrote such a note, most of them listing parts the request never named. `--note` itself is unchanged.
- `label` is optional on `layer add`, and its batch line shows only `id`. A row drawn without a label draws no name: a bar is empty, and a band has no text in its head. A file whose rows have no `label` reads and writes back the same.
- `layer look <row> box|band` now draws the row, with its name, even when no line ends at it: a row with boxes as a band around them, a row with no boxes as one bar. A row with no look and no line ending at it is still not drawn, so diagrams without looks draw as before. A bar drawn for its look alone is checked as any bar: a line that passes it is refused as `bar-crossed` unless `edge pass` lets it, and one between a group's first and last rows is refused as `bar-in-group`, with `layer look` among the commands that can change it.
- A line between neighbouring rows whose lower end was pushed aside now also runs straight past a line in the gap that it crossed anyway: before, any line across the gap at its lower end kept it bent twice.
- The help says how to use grafical, and `grafical guide` how common kinds of diagram are usually drawn. `grafical --help` lists every way to draw, one line each by the story it tells, such as a box beside a box, one path in order, or two parts kept apart, in place of the list of diagram types, and says what grafical does not draw. Each command's `--help` shows its line in a batch, so `apply --help` no longer lists 15 of the 60 operations. `grafical guide` lists guides for architecture, C4, infrastructure, dependency, data flow, event flow, flowchart, swimlane and state machine diagrams, and one on composition; `grafical guide <name>` prints one. Nothing tells an agent to read them.
- `grafical --help` names `group title` and `edge pass` in their groups' lines, which left them out.
- `grafical help <command>` is gone: a command's page is `grafical <command> --help`. A command grafical does not have is refused with the one it most likely means: `grafical help apply` gives `error: unknown-command` with `found: help` and `likely: --help`, `grafical "node add" --help` and `grafical node.add --help` give `likely: node add`, and `grafical nodee add --help` gives `likely: node`. Before, a wrong name with `--help` printed the entry page and exited 0, and `help <command>` exited 2 after printing the page.
- A line that ends at a lane, whose label finds no place beside what is left of the line clear of the other lines, is refused as `label-blocked`, naming the line and the lanes whose border lies across its label; the label was drawn across the border. A numbered line cut at a lane no longer has its label's text pushed past its place by the number's ring, across a lane's side or another line.
- `wall relabel <file> <id> [--label <text>]` changes the words on a wall's divider, or drops them without `--label` so that the wall is space alone; in a batch it is `wall.relabel`. Until now a wall's label could change only by removing the wall and adding it again.
- `group-split` names the box, its row, the innermost group whose members it stands between, and those two members (`between`).
- Command arguments are named as their batch fields: `group title <file> <id> [at]`, `group join <file> <group> <nodes...>`, `lane join`, `lane leave`, `column join` and `column leave` take `<members...>`, `edge emphasize` and `edge weight` take `<edges...>`, and `wall allow` takes `<allow...>`. Only the help's words change; the commands take the same values.

- The counts gain `bent=`, the lines drawn with more than two bends, each also a `bent-line` warning. Nothing is refused for it: it tells the agent which line its rows send round, which before only `check --all` measured.

- `group title <file> <group> <place>` (or `group label`) says where a group's label sits: `top-left`, `top` or `top-right` in its head, `bottom-left`, `bottom` or `bottom-right` in a foot below its last row, `left` or `right` beside its rows inside the box, or `outside-top`, `outside-bottom`, `outside-left` or `outside-right`, centred just outside the box; the file holds it as the group's `titleAt`, a batch as `group.title`, and `show` prints `title=<place>`. The label sits exactly there and takes only its own room; no box or line moves for it. A line that would run across it refuses the write as `title-blocked`, which names the lines in the way and, as `clear`, the places none comes near. Without a place the label is seated as before.
- `edge step <file> <id> --n <number>` numbers a line as a step of a process, from 1 to 99, and grafical draws the number in a small ring on the line, under the start of its label. Without `--n` the number is cleared; in a batch it is `edge.step`. `show` prints `[step <n>]` after the line, and `show --drawn` adds `step=<n>` to its record. A numbered line keeps its own port, and its label's text stands a little further from the line, so a picture with numbers grows a little taller. `check --all` reports a badge that touches anything as `step-collision`.
- A line that runs straight between two boxes centred one under the other now meets them at their centres more often: where other ports or the lines sharing its way had pushed it a little aside and nothing else would move or bend, it is put back.
- A row with no boxes may be a line's end, such as a gateway or a message bus: it is drawn as one 40 px bar across the picture with its name in it, and lines meet it straight past their other ends. It was refused as `empty-container`. `layer look <row> box|band` draws a bar or a band as a box or as a band; a bar is a box unless it says band. A line that passes a bar without ending at it is refused as `bar-crossed` unless `edge add --pass` or `edge pass` lets it; `bar-to-bar` and `bar-in-group` refuse a line between two bars and a bar inside a group's rows.
- `group order` moves the boxes of a group's inner groups with its own, as one block: two zones whose boxes all sit in subnets now swap, where they were refused as `no-shared-layer`. A group ordered beside itself, or beside a group around or inside it, is refused as `bad-reference`.
- A line between two boxes of one row may run above the row instead of under it, when fewer lines meet the boxes between its ends from above than from below: it then crosses fewer lines. It stays below where its box lies under a group's label, where a wall or a lane's border lies above the row, and where going above would bend another line or push a label out of its place.
- `group copy` copies a group with its inner groups, its boxes and the lines between them, every id with `--suffix`, before or after a group in each of its rows: a second zone, region or shard in one operation. `--outside` copies its lines to everything else too.
- `show --drawn` prints a `gutter` record for each pair of neighbouring rows, with the lines, crossings, tight pairs and crowded labels between them.
- The `counts` record gains `across=` (lines through a group holding neither of their ends), `tight=` (pairs of runs of two lines side by side under 12 px apart for over 100 px) and `crowded=` (line labels with under 8 px of room on two or more sides). `apply --help` describes them.
- `grafical try <file> <batch.yaml>...` takes several batches: one `before` record, then each batch's `ok`, `after` and `delta` records keyed by `batch=<file>`.
- An unknown option, a missing argument or an unknown command prints a block, such as `error: unknown-option` with `found: --bogus` (exit 2), instead of the parser's sentence.
- `node add` into a row that already has boxes, and `node move --layer`, need a place: `--first`, `--last`, `--before` or `--after`. Without one the write is refused as `missing-field`, with the row and its boxes. grafical no longer picks the slot.
- `layer tidy` and `diagram tidy` are removed. Compare orders with `grafical try` instead.
- The legend says only what the agent named: line styles and widths lose their default words ("call or read", "message", "optional", "related", "main path"), and a diagram that names nothing draws no legend. `show --drawn` prints a `legend` record with `text=-` for each style and width left unnamed, with the `weight` to name it by. `diagram legend --hide` is removed; an old file's `false` is read as no words.
- `note add --footnote` makes a note a footnote, held as `footnote: true` in the file; without it a note is a card. A card with no place beside its target no longer turns into a footnote by itself: the write is refused as `label-blocked` with `is: note`.
- A wall's label with no place along its wall clear of the lines across it is refused as `label-blocked` with `is: wall`, instead of moving across the picture, left of everything, or above or below the wall.
- A line's label, or a step's number, that no gap between rows on its way has room for no longer sends its line out round the crowd: the write is refused as `label-blocked`, which names the line, then the lines across the label, as `gutters` the gaps it tried, each `<row above>/<row below>`, and `node resize` among the commands that can change it. grafical never widens a box for its lines.
- A group's label with no place stated sits at the top, at the leftmost spot no line comes near, and no box or line moves for it: boxes no longer move apart for it, and lines no longer leave their boxes' centres to keep off it. Where a line comes near every spot, the write is refused as `title-blocked`, with the places no line comes near as `clear`, and the agent states one.
- Several lines may turn into one side of a box, each at its own height, the nearer from above entering higher: a second line into a side no longer bends twice into the box's top.
- The help teaches composing an architecture diagram: each system a group, the groups side by side with each entry above its stores; boxes that talk next to each other; a row that lines end at, drawn as one band, for what every request passes, such as a gateway; a queue as a box only when it is the story, otherwise its events as lines between the services; and side paths cut. To stay within the help's length, `grafical --help` no longer repeats what a refusal looks like: `grafical apply --help` says it.
- A line between two neighbours in a row runs straight across the gap with its label above it for labels up to 200 px wide, about thirty characters; the limit was 120 px, and a longer label sent the line round under the row.
- A line that ends at a row meets its band straight past its other end where nothing is in the way, instead of turning toward a box inside the band first.
- A label that must leave its best place no longer pushes another line across a fan-out: the place it takes is weighed by the crossings it truly forces.
- Two lines that leave one side of a box and turn into boxes on either side of it now both turn once: the second leaves a little off the side's middle, where it bent twice before.
- Two lines may now turn through the same gap between two boxes of a row, by their sides, when their runs along the row keep apart. A line that bent twice because the gap was taken now turns once.
- A line between neighbouring rows runs straight when the lower box's other ports, or a group label above it, push its lower end off the upper one: the upper end moves to meet it, where nothing else in the gutter runs across.
- A line's label no longer lies across the border of a group box, and `check --all` reports one that does as `text-crossing`, naming the group and the line.
- A line's label no longer lies across the border of a lane or a band either, and `check --all` reports one that does as `text-crossing`, naming the lane or the row and the line. A labelled line that crosses from one column lane into the next moves their shared side close to the first lane, and its label sits past the side, so a picture with column lanes may grow a little wider.
- Columns hold across rows: every box of a column now sits left of every box of the next column in any row, not only in its own row. A picture that uses columns may grow wider.
