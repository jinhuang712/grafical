# grafical Philosophy

> Written 2026-09-10.

This document sits above everything else in grafical. It is not a design, not a roadmap, not a list of features. Code, formats, and commands will change; these do not.

When a decision conflicts with this document, the decision is wrong.

## Structure decides the picture

Where things sit is not something the diagram says. It is what the diagram looks like, and it follows from what the diagram says.

Nothing else decides the picture. Not a hand, not taste, not a guess.

## Drawing is a series of discrete actions

An act of drawing is not a continuous adjustment. It is a sequence of discrete, atomic actions — one thing added, one thing connected, one thing regrouped — each of which either happens or does not.

There is no continuum to slide along. Nothing is approached, approximated, or edged toward.

## Less is more

The author is a model, and every distinction it has to make is a chance to get it wrong. The tool offers few, and the default answer to "should it also offer this?" is no.

What does earn a place holds three properties:

- **Few** — one intent, one way to say it.
- **Stable** — a name keeps its meaning.
- **Meaningful** — a choice produces the effect it names.

Stability is promised from the first release, not before.

## Moving pixels is banned

Not restricted, not discouraged, not a fallback — absent. There is no operation for it, no way to represent it, nothing to store. It is not a weaker kind of drawing. It is not drawing.

## Errors are not for the human

A defect is addressed to whoever produced the structure, and it stops there. A person looks at a diagram, never at a diagnostic.

---

These are few on purpose. If a decision cannot be traced back to one of them, reconsider the decision.
