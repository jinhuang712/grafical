## What the judges held against the drafts

1. **Crossings and tangled bottoms.** Lines from one service to many dependencies two rows down run as separate lines, with staircases and detours, not as one trunk with branches as in the target. This is engine-owned.
2. **Node order.** No draft reordered its rows. `layer.tidy` (D24) is in FEATURES.md but was never built, so Haiku could not call it. A trial that moves single nodes inside their rows while crossings drop, keeping groups and alignments valid, cuts crossings from 8, 7 and 6 to 2 each. It does not fix the bends: r1 rises from 4 to 6.
3. **Merged boxes that claim false edges.** "geo, i18n, auth" takes an edge from catalog-api, which only calls i18n. "MongoDB catalog, inventory" takes catalog-sync's write, but catalog-sync never writes inventory. The guide says to merge what a reader does not need apart, but it does not warn that a merged box claims every edge of every member. This is agent-owned, and the guide coaches it.
4. **A retry topic with no way back** (r3), and retry folded into the line topics (r1).
5. **A lopsided canvas** (r3): the top right is empty and the picture is 1096 px wide.

## Other findings
